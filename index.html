About this tracker

An unofficial monitor of active U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission rulemakings, with a focus on rules affecting new nuclear deployment, SMR siting, data center power procurement, and the operating fleet. Each rule is paired with a plain-language summary; the rules most relevant to KH client work additionally have a Kimley-Horn practice implication.

Maintained by the Kimley-Horn Nuclear Task Force. Not legal advice. Data is auto-scraped from NRC.gov every 6 hours; always consult primary sources before relying on this information for licensing or compliance decisions.
